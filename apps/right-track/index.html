Copyright, register, license, track, collect.

Right Track now guides a song and master from creator intake through ownership clearance, U.S. copyright filing preparation, rights registrations, licensing documents, usage tracking, invoices and royalty reconciliation.

Copyright exists automatically when an eligible original work is fixed. Federal registration and third-party rights registrations are only marked complete after authoritative confirmation. Right Track never fabricates filings, licenses, signatures or royalty balances.

Completion gate

A track is Rights Ready only after required ownership totals 100%, conflicts are resolved, required filing/registration evidence is recorded, and the intended license has all necessary approvals. External submissions remain pending until an authorized integration or verified filing receipt confirms them.
