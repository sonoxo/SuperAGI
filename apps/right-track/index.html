Copyright, register, license, track, collect.

Right Track guides a song and master from creator intake through ownership clearance, copyright filing preparation, rights registrations, licensing documents, usage tracking, invoices and royalty reconciliation.

Copyright exists automatically when an eligible original work is fixed. Federal registration and third-party registrations are marked complete only after authoritative confirmation. Right Track never fabricates filings, licenses, signatures or balances.

1 — Track intake & chain of title

Upload final master + lyrics/composition deposit
Capture writers, composers, performers, producers and publishers
Validate composition splits + master ownership to 100%
Capture ISRC, ISWC, UPC and IPI/CAE when available
Vault split sheets, producer agreements, samples, features and ownership evidence

2 — U.S. Copyright filing

Right Track prepares the claim packet, deposit checklist and metadata, then hands the rightsholder to the official Copyright Office registration system. The current public filing path is eCO; Right Track does not claim a private submission API that has not been authorized.

4 — License Builder

Generate clearance packets for mechanical/reproduction, synchronization, master-use and direct/custom licenses. Issuance is blocked while required owners or approvals are missing.

Terms: licensee, territory, media, term, exclusivity, fee, royalty, reporting, credit and permitted use
Composition-right clearance + master-right clearance tracked separately
Owner approval + signature/e-sign evidence required before Executed

Completion gate

A track becomes Rights Ready only after ownership totals 100%, conflicts are resolved, required authoritative filing/registration evidence is recorded, and the intended license has all necessary approvals.
